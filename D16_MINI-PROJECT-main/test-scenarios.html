Operational Test Scenarios

Each scenario resets the shared platform engine, executes real system actions, and reports its assertions step by step.

TEST 1

FIFO seat promotion

Cancel a confirmed passenger and verify that the oldest waitlisted passenger receives the same seat.

TEST 2

Non-overlapping platforms

Competing arrival windows must never overlap on the same physical platform.

TEST 3

Overlong train rejection

A 650m train must be rejected explicitly, not left waiting forever.

TEST 4

Delay reallocation

Extend an occupation and verify the train remains allocated or queued without double-booking.

TEST 5

Emergency promotion

When a platform frees, the emergency request must precede a scheduled request.
