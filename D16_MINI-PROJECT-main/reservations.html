Train Seat Reservation & Waitlist Management

Interactive booking grid for all 6 train services. Each train has a strictly enforced 10-passenger capacity. When a train reaches 10 confirmed seats, subsequent passengers are enqueued into a First-Come, First-Served (FIFO) waiting list. Cancelling any confirmed booking automatically promotes the front waiting passenger into the newly freed seat.
